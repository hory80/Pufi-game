Pufi disclaimer

Pufi supports learning and play. It does not replace a teacher, an educational assessment or a parent's judgement. Nothing on this page limits rights that applicable law does not allow us to exclude.

1. Educational purpose and learning outcomes

Pufi provides supplementary educational activities, primarily for children aged 6–8. Activities are not a certified curriculum, qualification, diagnostic tool or assessment of a child's abilities. Children learn at different speeds. We do not promise a particular reading level, school result or improvement within a fixed period. Stars, scores, belts and other rewards describe progress within the game, not a formal evaluation of the child.

2. Adult supervision and appropriate use

A parent or guardian should choose suitable activities, supervise voice and story-creation features, and manage permissions and any purchases. The parental gate reduces accidental access to adult-only features; it is not identity or age verification and does not replace supervision. Encourage regular breaks and comfortable volume. Do not use the app where looking at or interacting with a device would be unsafe.

3. Speech recognition and generated feedback

Speech recognition, pronunciation, syllable separation and automated feedback may be inaccurate. Results can vary with language, accent, background noise, system voices, device and connection. A missed or incorrectly recognised word does not establish that a child read incorrectly or has a learning difficulty. Adults should review feedback and correct misunderstandings. Depending on availability, speech recognition may involve Apple servers as explained in the privacy policy.

4. Content accuracy and family-created stories

We aim to make educational material accurate, but errors, simplified explanations, translation differences and outdated information can occur. World facts, maps, flags, monuments and other reference material are offered for general learning, not authoritative research. Please report suspected errors. Stories typed or dictated by a family are not reviewed or moderated by us; adults should check their suitability and avoid including sensitive personal information.

5. Availability, compatibility and local progress

We do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation, identical behaviour across every device or continued availability of a particular system voice or external service. Some functions require supported hardware, permissions or internet. Progress and custom content are primarily stored on the device, without a Pufi cloud account. Device loss, deletion, software changes or storage problems can affect local data. Manage device backups according to your needs; backups and restoration depend on your platform settings.

6. Purchases and virtual rewards

Any paid access and price must be shown in the App Store or the purchase confirmation before payment. This page does not create a subscription, change an existing purchase or remove any right to a remedy or refund. App Store purchases are processed by Apple. Stars, decorative items and daily gifts are game rewards, have no cash value, cannot be redeemed for money and do not represent financial prizes.

Third-party images, recordings and other materials remain subject to their respective rights and licences. Their presence does not imply endorsement by a person, organisation, Wikimedia, Apple or another rights holder. References to third-party services are informational; those providers control their own services and policies. This disclaimer does not grant permission to reuse third-party material or replace required attribution, licences or other permissions.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, Pufi is provided as available without additional promises of fitness for a particular learning outcome or uninterrupted operation. To that same extent, we exclude liability for indirect or consequential loss arising from use of the app.

Pufi sprijină învățarea prin joacă. Nu înlocuiește un profesor, o evaluare educațională sau aprecierea părintelui. Nicio prevedere de aici nu limitează drepturile care nu pot fi excluse potrivit legii aplicabile.

1. Scop educativ și rezultate

Pufi oferă activități educative suplimentare, în principal pentru copii de 6–8 ani. Nu reprezintă o programă certificată, o calificare, un instrument de diagnostic sau o evaluare a capacităților copilului. Copiii învață în ritmuri diferite. Nu promitem un anumit nivel de citire, rezultat școlar sau progres într-un termen fix. Stelele, scorurile, centurile și recompensele reflectă evoluția în joc, nu o evaluare formală a copilului.

2. Supraveghere și utilizare potrivită

Părintele sau tutorele alege activitățile potrivite, supraveghează funcțiile vocale și crearea poveștilor și gestionează permisiunile și eventualele cumpărături. Poarta pentru părinți reduce accesul accidental la funcțiile pentru adulți; nu verifică identitatea ori vârsta și nu înlocuiește supravegherea. Încurajați pauze regulate și un volum confortabil. Nu folosiți aplicația atunci când privirea sau interacțiunea cu dispozitivul ar fi nesigură.

3. Recunoaștere vocală și feedback automat

Recunoașterea vorbirii, pronunția, despărțirea în silabe și feedbackul automat pot avea erori. Rezultatele depind de limbă, accent, zgomot, vocile sistemului, dispozitiv și conexiune. Un cuvânt omis sau recunoscut greșit nu dovedește că un copil a citit greșit sau are dificultăți de învățare. Adulții trebuie să verifice feedbackul și să corecteze neînțelegerile.
